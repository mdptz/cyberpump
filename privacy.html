⚡ DATA PROTECTION

PRIVACY POLICY

Last Updated: July 2026

🔒 1. Local-First Philosophy

CYBERPUMP is designed around a strict "Local-First" privacy model. All your workout data (routines, sets, weights, notes, and diary logs) is stored exclusively on your local device. We do not operate external cloud servers to collect or retain your fitness data.

💾 2. Collected Data & Device Permissions

The application only requires access to essential hardware features necessary for executing your workouts:

  • Local Storage (LocalStorage / SQLite): Used to save your routines and workout diary history on your smartphone.
  • Notifications & Vibration: Used solely to alert you when rest timers and timebased/EMOM rounds expire.
  • Web Speech API (Text-To-Speech): Used to generate voice audio countdowns (3, 2, 1) during workout sets.

💳 3. In-App Purchases

Payments for the PREMIUM version are processed directly through the official infrastructure of Google Play Store and Apple App Store via RevenueCat SDK. CYBERPUMP never collects, accesses, or stores your personal banking information or credit card details.

📤 4. Data Sharing & Tracking

We do not sell, rent, share, or track any personal user data with third parties for advertising or analytical purposes. JSON export files created via the "Share & Export" feature are transferred only when explicitly initiated by you.

🗑️ 5. Data Deletion & Control

Since your data resides entirely on your local device, you maintain full control over its deletion at all times:

  • By tapping "Restore Preset Workouts" inside the app's Settings menu.
  • By clearing the app storage/cache from your Android device settings.
  • By uninstalling the application from your device.
