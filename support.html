❓ Frequently Asked Questions

  • How do I unlock CYBERPUMP Premium? You can unlock Premium with a one-time purchase from App Settings or by tapping "Unlock Unlimited" on the paywall modal.
  • How do I restore my purchases? Go to Settings > Restore Purchases within the app to restore your PRO access on a new device.
  • Where is my workout data stored? All your routines and workout logs are stored locally and securely on your smartphone device.
